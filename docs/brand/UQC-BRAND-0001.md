> Brand research, art direction, vector identity, and usage guidance only. This document does not implement the landing page or authorize development.

## 1. Brand objective

Create a durable identity for Unbreakable Quality Cutz that communicates precision, reliability, confidence, neighborhood trust, and modern convenience. The system must work at storefront scale, in a mobile booking interface, on social profiles, and at favicon size without relying on barber-industry clichés.

## 2. Research synthesis

The strongest premium service identities share five traits:

1. A simple, ownable silhouette that survives reduction.
2. Typography with disciplined spacing rather than decorative effects.
3. One memorable accent color used selectively.
4. Authentic photography that proves the work.
5. A modular system rather than one oversized logo.

Common category weaknesses include clipper illustrations, barber poles, crowns, shields, distressed scripts, crowded badges, and visual claims of luxury unsupported by service clarity. UQC should instead feel engineered, direct, and grounded.

## 3. Selected creative direction — Unbreakable Standard

**Concept:** A modern neighborhood institution built around clean execution and dependable service.

**Design language:**

- Geometric letter construction
- Strong verticals and clipped corners
- Controlled negative space
- Condensed display typography
- Warm neutral surfaces
- Signal Amber used only for action and emphasis
- Documentary Black grooming photography

**Rationale:** This direction aligns with the approved product blueprint, performs well in digital interfaces, supports a premium but accessible neighborhood position, and avoids trend-dependent barber graphics.

## 4. Logo concept

The core mark is a custom `UQC` monogram.

- The outer `U` represents strength, continuity, and the chair/customer relationship.
- The central negative-space `Q` suggests precision and a directional cut.
- The terminating diagonal creates motion without using a literal razor or clipper.
- Squared geometry reinforces reliability and technical craft.

The mark must never be described as a barber crown, shield, or clipper icon.

## 5. Approved logo family

1. **Primary horizontal lockup:** monogram plus full business name.
2. **Stacked lockup:** monogram above wordmark for square placements.
3. **Monogram:** compact `UQC` mark for navigation, social avatars, uniforms, and signage.
4. **Wordmark:** full business name without symbol for narrow applications.
5. **One-color mark:** solid black, white, or approved single ink.
6. **Reverse mark:** Bone/white artwork on Carbon or Forge.
7. **Watermark:** monogram at 4–10% opacity only behind noncritical content.
8. **Favicon/app mark:** simplified monogram without small text.

Vector source files are stored in `docs/assets/brand/`.

## 6. Color system

| Token | Value | Primary use |
|---|---|---|
| Carbon | `#121212` | Primary dark background and high-emphasis text |
| Forge | `#222426` | Secondary dark surface |
| Concrete | `#6F7477` | Supporting text, rules, metadata |
| Bone | `#F3EFE7` | Warm light background |
| White | `#FFFFFF` | Reverse text and clean surfaces |
| Signal Amber | `#D98A24` | Primary action, active state, key accent |
| Success | `#2F7D4A` | Confirmed/completed state |
| Danger | `#B63A32` | Error, cancellation, destructive state |

Signal Amber must not become a full-page background or decorative wash. Text/background combinations require WCAG 2.2 AA validation before implementation.

## 7. Typography direction

**Display:** `Barlow Condensed`, `Roboto Condensed`, or another licensed/open-source condensed sans with strong uppercase performance.  
**UI and body:** `Geist`, `Inter`, or an equivalent variable sans.  
**Numerals:** Tabular numerals for prices, appointment times, and dashboard metrics.

Rules:

- Headlines may use uppercase with deliberate tracking.
- Body copy remains sentence case.
- Minimum routine body size is 16 px.
- Avoid script fonts, faux chrome effects, outlines, and excessive italics.

Final font selection remains subject to licensing, performance, and visual testing.

## 8. Spacing and clear space

Use the monogram stem width as unit `x`.

- Minimum clear space around any logo: `1x`.
- Minimum clear space around the primary lockup: `1.5x` vertically and `1x` horizontally.
- Do not place the mark against busy haircut photography without a controlled solid field.
- Do not crowd the logo with booking badges, phone numbers, or partner marks.

## 9. Minimum sizes

- Full horizontal lockup: 180 px digital / 38 mm print.
- Stacked lockup: 96 px digital / 25 mm print.
- Monogram: 24 px digital / 8 mm print.
- Favicon: use simplified mark at 16, 32, and 48 px.

At sizes below 24 px, use only the simplified monogram.

## 10. Icon system

The custom icon family uses:

- 24×24 view box
- 1.75 px default stroke
- Rounded line caps with restrained corner radius
- No filled decorative backgrounds
- Clear silhouettes at 16–24 px
- Shared optical weight with the monogram

Required service and interface icons include haircut, fade, beard, razor, hair system, consultation, education, booking, calendar, location, phone, gallery, review, hours, parking, account, dashboard, analytics, settings, notification, security, email, and SMS.

The symbol sprite is stored at `docs/assets/icons/uqc-icon-sprite.svg`.

## 11. Landing-page application

### Header

Use the horizontal lockup on desktop and the monogram on compact mobile navigation. Keep the booking CTA visually separate from the logo.

### Hero

Use the reverse or light primary lockup over a controlled Carbon field. Authentic photography should show Turk performing actual work; generated imagery must not be presented as portfolio evidence.

### Services

Use service icons only as navigation aids. Service photography and plain-language descriptions remain the primary proof.

### Booking

Use icons for steps, status, and confirmation—not as replacements for text labels. Active and primary actions use Signal Amber.

### Footer

Use the one-color or reverse horizontal lockup with sufficient clear space. Metaphor may appear as a discreet development credit only where contractually approved.

## 12. Social and small-format use

- Avatar: monogram centered on Carbon with Bone or white mark.
- Story highlight covers: one icon per cover, no text below 48 px.
- Open Graph: primary lockup, authentic image, short approved message.
- Watermark: monogram only, positioned away from haircut details.

## 13. Prohibited uses

- Stretching, skewing, rotating, or outlining the mark
- Changing individual logo colors outside approved variants
- Adding gradients, chrome, bevels, glow, drop shadows, crowns, clippers, poles, or flames
- Rebuilding the logo with a substitute font
- Placing the detailed wordmark at favicon size
- Using generated hair transformations as proof of client results
- Claiming “Master Barber” until credentials are verified and approved
