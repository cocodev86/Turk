## Required export family after approval

The approved master should later be exported by the implementation/design-production phase as:

- Primary horizontal SVG, PDF, EPS, PNG
- Stacked SVG, PDF, EPS, PNG
- Monogram SVG and transparent PNG
- One-color black and white variants
- Reverse variants
- 16, 32, and 48 px favicon PNGs plus `favicon.ico`
- 180 px Apple touch icon
- 192 and 512 px PWA icons
- 512 px maskable icon with safe-area padding
- 1080×1080 social avatar
- 1200×630 Open Graph artwork
- Print-ready CMYK/Pantone specification after vendor testing

No raster or application exports are authorized in this documentation phase.

## Approval checklist

- [ ] Owner selects and approves identity direction
- [ ] Business name and mark receive appropriate conflict review
- [ ] Monogram remains legible at 16, 24, and 32 px
- [ ] One-color reproduction passes
- [ ] Reverse reproduction passes
- [ ] Color combinations pass WCAG 2.2 AA where used in UI
- [ ] Storefront/signage test passes at intended distance
- [ ] Social avatar test passes
- [ ] Service icons are distinguishable without text-only dependence
- [ ] Landing-page header and hero mockups are approved
- [ ] Supporting credentials and claims are verified

## Production control

These files are proposed design masters. They must not be copied into runtime application directories until the Experience Design and Architecture gates approve their use. Approved production derivatives should preserve a single authoritative vector source and record version, approval, export date, and owner.
